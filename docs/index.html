Optional YouTube connection

Your channel. Your choice to share.

Framebit uses YouTube API Services to upload a clip only when you choose Upload. Connecting your Google account lets Framebit identify your YouTube channel and upload the edited video with the title, description, visibility and audience setting you choose.

YouTube access is currently being prepared for public availability and may be limited to approved test accounts. No Google account is needed to record, edit or save clips locally.

See our privacy policy for permissions, local token storage, disconnecting and removing Google access. Use of the YouTube feature is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
