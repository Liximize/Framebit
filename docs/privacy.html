Your data and your choices

Privacy policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

Framebit is maintained by Liximize. This policy covers the Framebit Windows app and this project website. Contact: Liximize.Dev@outlook.com.

Recording and local data

Framebit processes screen images and the audio sources you enable to provide recordings, replay clips, screenshots and editing. It stores recordings and screenshots in your configured media folders. App settings, library metadata, favorites, logs and caches are stored locally. Temporary replay and editing files are also created locally. Recording or editing a clip does not automatically upload it to the developer or to YouTube.

The reviewed app does not include a developer-operated analytics or advertising service. Third-party services and Windows components may process their own operational data under their policies.

Optional Google and YouTube access

Framebit uses YouTube API Services. When you choose to connect, Google handles sign-in in your browser; Framebit does not receive your Google password. Framebit requests these permissions:

  • Upload your videos (youtube.upload): upload the edited clip and the title, description, visibility and audience setting you submit.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): request your channel information so the app can display the connected channel name. The current integration uses this permission for channel identification, not to download your viewing history.

Framebit displays the connected channel name and uses access and refresh tokens to make authorized requests. Tokens and pending upload-session information are stored on your device, encrypted using Windows data protection for your Windows account. Channel information is used to identify the connection in the editor. The developer does not operate a server that receives your Google tokens or channel information.

What is shared when you upload

When you choose Upload, the selected edited video and its submitted metadata are sent directly from your device to Google/YouTube. Upload progress and resumable-session information are processed to complete or retry the upload. An upload may create a local prepared export; local working files and caches are separate from videos stored by YouTube. YouTube processes and retains uploaded material under its own policies.

Framebit does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising or AI model training. Framebit’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting, revoking access and deletion

  • Disconnect in Framebit: open YouTube in the editor and choose Disconnect. This removes locally saved access/refresh tokens and pending upload-session information. It does not revoke the Google Account permission or delete uploaded videos.
  • Revoke Google access: remove Framebit from your Google Account permissions. Also choose Disconnect in Framebit to remove its local saved connection data.
  • Delete videos already uploaded: manage or delete them in YouTube Studio. Deleting a local recording or disconnecting Framebit does not delete a YouTube upload.
  • Delete local media: use Framebit’s Trash controls or Windows. Items moved to Framebit Trash are subject to its seven-day retention; you may permanently delete them sooner. Local app settings, logs and caches remain until removed or cleaned by the app. Uninstalling should not be relied on to delete recordings or all app data.

You can request help with local data removal by email. The developer cannot remotely delete files on your device or videos in your YouTube account. If you voluntarily send a support message or logs, the developer receives what you include and uses it to respond and investigate; request deletion by email. Do not send passwords, tokens or private recordings in public issue reports.

Updates, website and other services

Update checks and downloads contact GitHub. Visiting this website contacts GitHub Pages. These providers receive normal network information such as your IP address and request details. This static website does not add analytics scripts, advertising, tracking cookies or external font requests. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Google authentication and YouTube uploads are governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Microsoft runtime components may operate under Microsoft’s policies. Following an external link takes you to the named service.

Security and policy changes

Local encryption reduces exposure of saved tokens but cannot guarantee security on a compromised device. Keep your Windows account secure. This policy will be updated when data practices change; the effective date above identifies the current version. Contact the maintainer with privacy questions.
