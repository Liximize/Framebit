# Getting started

## Install

Download the setup EXE from the [official Releases page](https://github.com/Liximize/Framebit/releases). Choose your installation folder and allow the required Microsoft runtime setup to finish. The setup EXE installs the app; the release's JSON, signature and checksum files support verification and updating.

## Choose your capture settings

In **Settings**, review your save folders, microphone, audio sources, hotkeys and startup options. Choose a capture resolution, frame rate, quality level and replay length before starting the buffer. Higher settings and longer replays need more resources and temporary disk space.

Start the replay buffer on **Home**. It captures the displayed desktop. Detecting a game helps organize saves and separate audio; it does not restrict video capture to that game's window. Use the save-replay button or your configured hotkey to keep recent footage. A newly started buffer needs time to accumulate footage.

Use normal recording for a longer session and the screenshot control or hotkey for a still image. Keep private windows and content you do not want to capture off the recorded screen.

## Find and edit your captures

Open **Clips** or **Screenshots** and select a game filter. Star an item to include it in **Favorites**. Deleted items can be managed from **Trash**.

Open a clip in the editor, adjust its timeline and available audio controls, then choose **Finish editing**. The edited copy returns to the library with its finished status; the source clip is kept. Imported videos may have a single mixed audio track rather than Framebit's separate tracks.

## Background recording

Closing the window can send Framebit to the system tray, depending on your settings. Use the tray menu to reopen it or choose **Exit Framebit** to stop the app. Review startup and tray preferences in Settings.

## Updates

Open **Settings → Updates** to check manually. When offered an update, download it and choose **Update and restart**. Finish editing and saving first. Restarting the recorder discards unsaved replay history, while saved clips remain in their folders.

For problems, see [Troubleshooting](TROUBLESHOOTING.md).
